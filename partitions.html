The Partitions of Poland

The Partitions of Poland were a series of events that changed the borders of Poland. They took place in 1772, 1793 and 1795. Poland was divided between Russia, Prussia and Austria. After the third partition, Poland disappeared from the map of Europe for 123 years. During this time, many Polish people continued to fight for independence. Poland regained its independence in 1918.
