World War II for Denmark

During World War II, Denmark was occupied by Germany in 1940. The occupation lasted until 1945, when Germany surrendered. The Danish government continued to work for some time under German control, while resistance movements also developed. Many Danish people helped the resistance and supported the Allied countries. Denmark was liberated in May 1945. The war had a major impact on Danish society and history.
