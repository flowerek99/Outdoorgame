Wieliczka

The Wieliczka Salt Mine is one of the most famous historical places in Poland. It is located near Kraków and was opened in the Middle Ages. Miners worked there for hundreds of years to produce salt. The mine has many underground tunnels, chambers and sculptures made from salt. It is also famous for its underground chapel. Today, it is a popular tourist attraction and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
