John Paul II

John Paul II was a famous Polish pope and an important religious figure. He was born in 1920 in Wadowice, Poland. In 1978, he became the first Polish pope and the first non-Italian pope in many years. He travelled to many countries and met millions of people around the world. He supported peace and encouraged people to help others. He died in 2005 and is remembered as one of the most famous Polish people.
