Autonomous Territories of Denmark

Greenland and the Faroe Islands are autonomous territories within the Kingdom of Denmark. Greenland is the largest island in the world and is located between the Arctic and Atlantic Oceans. The Faroe Islands are a group of 18 islands in the North Atlantic Ocean. Both places have their own governments and have a large degree of autonomy. Greenland is famous for its huge ice sheet, while the Faroe Islands are known for their mountains and cliffs. Both territories are connected to Denmark but have their own unique cultures.
