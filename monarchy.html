Political system in Denmark

Denmark is a constitutional monarchy and a parliamentary democracy. The country has a king as the head of state, but his role is mainly formal. The government is led by a prime minister, who is the head of government. The Danish Parliament is called the Folketing and has 179 members. Citizens vote for political parties and members of parliament. Denmark is also a member of the European Union.
