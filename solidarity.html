Solidarity

Solidarity was a Polish trade union and social movement founded in 1980. It began at the Gdańsk Shipyard and was led by Lech Wałęsa. The movement fought for better working conditions and greater political freedom. In 1981, the communist government introduced martial law and banned Solidarity. The movement continued to operate underground and became an important part of the changes of 1989. These changes helped lead to the end of communist rule in Poland.
