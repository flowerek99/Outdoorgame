Niels Bohr

Niels Bohr was a famous Danish physicist and scientist. He was born in 1885 in Copenhagen, Denmark. He made important discoveries about atoms and how they work. His work helped develop modern physics and our understanding of the atomic world. He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1922. He died in 1962, but his ideas are still important in science.
