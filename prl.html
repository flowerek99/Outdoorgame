The Polish People's Republic was the name of Poland from 1952 to 1989. During this period, Poland was a communist country closely connected to the Soviet Union. The government controlled much of everyday life, the economy and the media. People had limited political freedom and there were shortages of some products. In 1989, major political changes ended communist rule in Poland. This period is an important part of modern Polish history.
