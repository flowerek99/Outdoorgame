Polish Tatars

Tatars have lived in Poland for many centuries. The first larger groups arrived in the Polish-Lithuanian lands in the Middle Ages. Many of them served as soldiers in the armies of the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth. Polish Tatars kept their Muslim religion and many of their traditions. Today, there are still Tatar communities in Poland, especially in Podlaskie Voivodeship. Their culture is an important part of Poland's history and diversity.
