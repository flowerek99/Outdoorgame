Harald Bluetooth

Harald Bluetooth was a king of Denmark who lived in the 10th century. He ruled Denmark and also controlled parts of Norway. He is famous for helping to unite Denmark and for introducing Christianity to the country. He also built several important fortifications. The modern Bluetooth technology was named after him because he was known for bringing people together. His nickname became the symbol of the technology.
