Westerplatte

Westerplatte is a small peninsula in Gdańsk, Poland. It is famous because it was one of the first places attacked during World War II. On 1 September 1939, German forces attacked the Polish military garrison there. The Polish soldiers defended Westerplatte for seven days despite being heavily outnumbered. Their defence became an important symbol of Polish resistance. The attack on Westerplatte is often associated with the beginning of World War II.
