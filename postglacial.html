Postglacial Landscape

A postglacial landscape is an area shaped by glaciers during the Ice Age. Glaciers moved across the land and changed its surface. They created lakes, hills, valleys and other interesting landforms. Northern Poland has many examples of this type of landscape. There are also many lakes and forests in these areas. Today, these regions are popular places for tourism and outdoor activities.
