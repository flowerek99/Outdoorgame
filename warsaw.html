Warsaw

Warsaw is the capital and largest city of Poland. It is located in the eastern part of the country, on the Vistula River. The city has a long and interesting history and was heavily destroyed during World War II. After the war, Warsaw was rebuilt and became an important European city. Today, it is famous for its Old Town, the Royal Castle and the Palace of Culture and Science. Warsaw is also an important center of culture, business and education.
