Battle of Grunwald

On 15 July 1410, one of the biggest battles of medieval Europe took place. The battle was fought between the Teutonic Order and the Polish-Lithuanian alliance. The battle was fierce, and around 45,000 men were fighting that day. The fighting lasted for several hours and both sides suffered heavy losses. The Polish-Lithuanian army won the battle, and the Teutonic Order was weakened after this defeat. It was an important victory for Poland and Lithuania.
