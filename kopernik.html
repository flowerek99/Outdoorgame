Nicolaus Copernicus

Nicolaus Copernicus was a famous Polish astronomer and scientist. He was born in 1473 in Toruń, Poland. He is best known for the idea that the Earth and other planets move around the Sun. His theory changed the way people understood the universe. He spent many years studying astronomy and writing about his ideas. He died in 1543, but his discoveries are still important today.
