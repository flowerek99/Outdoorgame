Bornholm

After World War II, the Danish island of Bornholm was occupied by Soviet forces. The Soviet occupation began in May 1945, after the German troops on the island surrendered. The Soviet soldiers stayed on Bornholm for about eleven months. Denmark wanted the Soviet forces to leave the island and return it to Danish control. In April 1946, the Soviet troops finally left Bornholm. The island then returned fully under Danish control.
