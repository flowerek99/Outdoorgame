Festung Breslau

Festung Breslau was the German name for Wrocław during the final months of World War II. In 1945, the city was declared a fortress by the German authorities. German forces defended the city against the advancing Soviet army. The fighting lasted for several months and caused major destruction in Wrocław. The German garrison surrendered on 6 May 1945, shortly before the end of the war in Europe. After the war, Wrocław became part of Poland.
