Władysław Anders

Władysław Anders was a famous Polish general and military commander. He was born in 1892 in Poland. During World War II, he became the commander of the Polish II Corps. His soldiers fought against German forces in Italy, including in the famous Battle of Monte Cassino. After the war, Anders stayed in exile and lived in Great Britain. He died in 1970 in London and was buried at the Polish cemetery at Monte Cassino.
