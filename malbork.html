Malbork Castle

Malbork Castle is a large medieval castle in northern Poland. The castle was built mainly from brick and is the largest brick castles in the world. It was built by the Teutonic Order in the 13th and 14th centuries. It became the headquarters of the Grand Master of the Teutonic Order in 1309. Today, it is a popular tourist attraction and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is an important example of medieval architecture.
