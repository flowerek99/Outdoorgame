LEGO

LEGO is a famous Danish company that makes construction toys. It was founded in 1932 in Denmark by Ole Kirk Christiansen. The company became famous for its small colorful plastic bricks that can be connected together. People can use LEGO bricks to build many different objects and structures. LEGO sets are popular with children and adults around the world. Today, LEGO is one of the most famous toy brands in the world.
