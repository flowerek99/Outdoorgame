Tycho Brahe

Tycho Brahe was a famous Danish astronomer and scientist. He was born in 1546 in Denmark and became interested in astronomy at a young age. He made very accurate observations of the stars and planets without using a telescope. His observations helped other scientists learn more about the universe. He worked with Johannes Kepler, who later used his observations to study the movement of planets. Tycho Brahe died in 1601, but his work was very important for the development of astronomy.
