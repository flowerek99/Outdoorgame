Oświęcim

Oświęcim is a town in southern Poland. During World War II, Nazi Germany established the Auschwitz concentration and extermination camp near the town. More than one million people were murdered there, most of them Jews. Today, the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial and Museum is located there. It is an important place of remembrance and education about the Holocaust. The town is also known by its German name, Auschwitz.
