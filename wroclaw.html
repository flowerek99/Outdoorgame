Wrocław

Wrocław is one of the biggest cities in Poland. It is located in western Poland, on the Oder River. The city has a long and complicated history and belonged to different countries in the past. Wrocław is famous for its beautiful Old Town, many bridges and small dwarf statues. Today, it is an important city for culture, education and business. It is also one of the most popular tourist cities in Poland.
