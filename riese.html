Riese

Riese was a large German construction project during World War II. It was built in the Owl Mountains in Lower Silesia, which is now part of Poland. The project included many underground tunnels and buildings. Thousands of prisoners were forced to work on the construction. The exact purpose of the complex is still not completely known. Today, some parts of Riese are open to tourists.
